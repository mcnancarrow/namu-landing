About Miyoung

Rooted in Wellness,
Guided by Purpose

Miyoung believes that how you feel in your body and how you live in your space are deeply connected. As a photographer and wellness practitioner, she creates images that honour both — capturing people in their most authentic, powerful state.

Her work spans fitness and body photography, wellness portraits, lifestyle content, and property photography for real estate — all approached with the same mindful intention and artistic care.
